---

Weekly job (Tuesday morning, Bermuda time): finish the deals half of Matt's "Bermuda Hub" and publish the Hub. The Hub is one published Claude artifact at https://claude.ai/artifact/AJ9V7V5dZhX7u4V8ttbP6d holding the what's on planner and the grocery and retail deals page behind a switch. Since November 2026 everything that can be scripted runs on Matt's laptop every morning at 6am from C:\Users\Matt\Documents\BermudaHub (see its README.md): the deals collector pulls every shelf price and downloads the flyer images, an OCR module (deals\bgd\ocr.py, EasyOCR plus Tesseract) reads the Lindo's and Supermart flyers into deals\data\flyers\lindos.json and supermart.json, a Facebook module (deals\bgd\facebook.py, Playwright, signed out) reads every store, farm, shop and restaurant page on Mondays into deals\data\facebook_latest.json, and run.py builds site\deals.html, site\planner.html and site\hub.html. Your job is only what a script cannot do: check the OCR flyer read, summarise the Facebook text, write the commentary, rebuild the deals page and the Hub offline, and publish. Matt shops on Tuesdays or Wednesdays. Prices are BMD. Work without asking questions; he is not watching. Do not use dashes as sentence punctuation in anything you write. Keep token use low: no browser session at all; everything comes from the laptop files plus two WebFetch calls.

STEP 2. Flyers. The OCR has already written lindos.json and supermart.json when it could. For each store: open the _ocr.json file; items with "conf" below 0.5 are ones where the two price readings disagreed. If the OCR file for the current flyer has at least 15 items and they look like products (not headings, store hours or wine lists), keep it and only check the flagged items: stage the flyer image named in "ocr.images" (Lindo's images are under deals\flyers\lindos\<post slug>\, Supermart under deals\flyers\supermart\), crop a strip around the flagged product with Python Pillow, Read the strip and correct the price or name in <store>.json; set "by" to "ocr+check" and "read" to today. If the OCR produced no items, fewer than 15, or mostly junk (Supermart's single tall image usually fails and the Lindo's pass has returned headings), keep the existing hand read if it still covers the posts in lindos_posts.json and transcribe only what is new: Read the image (crop into horizontal strips with Pillow if it is tall), and write <store>.json as {"source", "valid", "read", "by": "claude", "note", "items": [{"section", "item", "unit", "regular", "price"}]} using lindos_posts.json "post" slugs (newest first, space separated) or supermart_flyer.json "range" as "source". Remove lines whose flyer "valid until" date has passed (check the top strip of the image when unsure). Skip files named *Monthly* or *advert*. Sections: Meat, Seafood, Deli, Dairy, Frozen, Produce, Bakery, Grocery, Drinks, Household, Personal care, Pet, LNL bulk. Masters, Phoenix, P-Tech, Annex Toys: promo_images.json lists promotion images and whether each is new; stage only the new ones, Read them, and turn anything with a price or an end date into rdeals cards in editorial.json.

STEP 3. Facebook, from the laptop file. deals\data\facebook_latest.json is {"read", "tiers", "pages": [{"name", "url", "group" (grocery, farms, retail, eats), "tier", "status" (ok, login, notfound, empty, error), "posted" (ISO date or null), "text", "lines", "shot"}], "no_url": [names]}. If the file is missing or "read" is more than 8 days old, say so in the summary, carry last week's notes forward and do not open a browser. Treat "login" as "could not read" and "notfound" as a miss (after three recorded misses mark the shop "parked"). A "shot" is a screenshot of an image-only post under deals\flyers\facebook\; stage and Read only the ones for grocery stores, Price Rite and shops that run sales, and only when the text is empty. Also WebFetch https://specialityinn.bm/specials-promotions/ and http://www.latrattoria.bm/specials for the two restaurant specials pages. Price Rite flash sale prices, if the post has them, go in editorial.json "all_extra" as rows ["prc", "Flash sale (Wed to Sat)", item, size, regular or null, price].
Tiers come from config.json "facebook" (each page has "tier": weekly, monthly or parked; the collector reads weekly pages every Monday, monthly ones on the first Monday of the month and parked ones on the first Monday of January, April, July and October). When a shop's newest post is over 30 days old move it to monthly, over a year to parked, and a monthly shop that posts again goes back to weekly: make those changes in config.json "facebook" and commit config.json with the other files in STEP 4. A page in "no_url" has no address on file; carry its last note and say so.
Write what you read into editorial.json: farms (cards {n, when, t}), others (grocery store cards), rdeals (retail offers with a future end date; drop expired ones), rshops (update each shop's "when" and "t"; keep every shop and record any newly found page URL in "t"), edeals (eats offers and notices with a date), estanding (standing specials and the two delivery apps; sargasso_stores.json shows which restaurants are listed and is_closed, so note a newly closed or newly listed place in eplaces), eplaces (corrections only). Also write prose: ld_note and sm_note (one paragraph each for the header, from the flyer files; say the flyer was machine read and checked), staples_overall (two or three sentences on who wins the staples table this week, using staples.json), farms_sub, others_sub, rshops_sub (how many pages read by tier, newly found or parked, anything unreadable), eats_sub. Write "best": 8 to 12 cards {s: store code, p: price, u: unit, n: item, w: the working} comparing across stores using marketplace_ad.json, the two flyer files and staples.json, and "vs": head to head rows [item, storeA, "price (detail)", storeB, "price (detail)", winner code] where a Lindo's or Supermart flyer item matches a MarketPlace ad or shelf item. Set "asof" to today's ISO date. Store codes: mp MarketPlace, ld Lindo's, sm Supermart, ml Miles, pr Pronto, prc Price Rite. Standing notes confirmed by Matt: MarketPlace 5% off Tuesdays AND Wednesdays (never Wednesday only); Lindo's 5% off Tuesdays and Wednesdays through 2026; Supermart specials Wednesday to Sunday; Arnold's 15% off first Tuesday of the month; the Botanical Gardens farmers' market Saturdays November to May; J&J Produce reopens in November at Astwood Park; Amaral Farms fall market Saturdays 6:30am to noon, Middle Road, Devonshire.
